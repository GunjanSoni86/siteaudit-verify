Privacy Policy

Effective: May 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Snag List - Audit & Reporter (the "App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

1.1 Account Information

  • Email address — used to create your account and authenticate you.
  • Display name — shown in the App and on your reports.

1.2 User-Generated Content

  • Projects, reports, and issues — including titles, descriptions, dates, statuses, comments, and metadata you enter.
  • Photos and images — including cover photos, issue photos, signatures, and company logos you upload.
  • Settings and preferences — including company details, branding, report templates, and customization.

1.3 Subscription Information

  • Purchase records — transaction identifiers, product IDs, purchase and expiration dates returned by Apple after each subscription event.
  • We do not collect or store your payment card details. All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store.

1.4 Usage Data and Analytics

  • Anonymous usage events (which screens you visit, which features you use).
  • Device information (model, iOS version, app version, language).
  • Crash reports and diagnostic logs (in aggregated, non-personally-identifiable form).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the App and its features.
  • Sync your data across your devices when you sign in.
  • Process and manage subscription purchases.
  • Send you transactional emails (such as account verification and password reset).
  • Analyze usage trends to improve the App, fix bugs, and develop new features.
  • Respond to your support inquiries.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the App. Each service has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — handles all subscription purchases and renewals. Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Appwrite — cloud backend for storing your account, projects, and synced data. Appwrite Privacy Policy.
  • Mixpanel — anonymous usage analytics. Mixpanel Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase — remote configuration and crash reporting. Firebase Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on cloud infrastructure operated by our service providers (Appwrite). We take reasonable industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls on stored data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account remains active. When you delete your account from within the App (Settings → Delete Account), we delete your account information and user-generated content from our cloud backend. Anonymous usage analytics may be retained in aggregated form for longer periods for product improvement purposes.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — update any inaccurate information through the App's settings.
  • Deletion — delete your account and associated data at any time from the App's settings, or by emailing us.
  • Portability — request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdrawal of consent — stop using the App and delete your account.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by professionals and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than the country in which you reside. These countries may have data protection laws that are different from the laws of your country. By using the App, you consent to such transfers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Effective" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated through the App or via email.
